About BMW Pretoria - Official Dealer Since 1980

Welcome to BMW Pretoria, your trusted BMW models dealer in Gauteng. We are located at 123 Church Street, Pretoria. Visit our Contact Page for Map or Book a Test Drive today.

1980-1995: Started as small BMW service centre in Pretoria CBD with 5 employees. Focused on BMW 3 Series E30 models.

1996-2010: Expanded to full dealership, added BMW X Series, built new showroom on Church Street. Won BMW SA Dealer of the Year 2005, 2009.

2011-2026: Now 45 employees, servicing over 2,000 BMWs yearly. Specializing in BMW M Performance, iX Electric models, and approved used BMWs. Member of RMI and NAAMSA.

Our journey reflects Sheer Driving Pleasure for Pretoria drivers.
